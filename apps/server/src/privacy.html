Privacy notice: appointment assistant

Version 2026-10-v1. This draft must be reviewed by a lawyer before go-live (docs/DATA_POLICY.md §7).

Who we are

The clinic is the Data Fiduciary for the information below. Contact the grievance officer using the details shared in the WhatsApp chat ("privacy" or "complaint").

What we keep

Your WhatsApp number, the names of the people you book for (optionally year of birth and gender, to tell family members apart), appointment dates, times, tokens and visit type (new, follow-up or show reports), and your consent record.

What we never keep

Symptoms, health conditions, medicines, reports or images you send, voice notes or their transcripts, or the text of your messages. Health matters are discussed with the doctor during your consultation.

Why

To book, remind, manage the waiting queue and send follow-up reminders. Legal basis: your consent.

Who processes it

Meta (WhatsApp, to deliver messages), Amazon Web Services in India (hosting; Amazon Bedrock to understand booking requests; Amazon Transcribe for voice notes). These providers do not keep the content of understanding or transcription requests.

How long

Message records: 90 days. Visit details are anonymised after 180 days. Profiles are deleted after 12 months of inactivity.

Your rights

Write "my data" to see what we keep, "delete my data" to erase it, "stop" to stop reminders. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

A parent or guardian books for, and consents on behalf of, a child.
