ClinicQ Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-v2 · Effective 14 October 2026

ClinicQ is a WhatsApp appointment and queue assistant for doctors' clinics in India. It is operated by Incolo Systems ("Incolo", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information ClinicQ collects, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and how you can see or delete it. It applies to:

  • Patients and family members who message a clinic's ClinicQ WhatsApp number or open its booking page, and
  • Clinics and their staff who use the ClinicQ dashboard and connect their WhatsApp Business Account to ClinicQ through Meta.

1. Who is responsible

  • Patient information: the clinic you message is the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Incolo processes this information only on the clinic's behalf and on its instructions, as its Data Processor.
  • Clinic and staff account information, and information we receive from Meta when a clinic connects WhatsApp: Incolo is responsible for it.

2. Information we collect

From patients (through WhatsApp and the booking page)

  • Your WhatsApp number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • The names of the people you book for, how they are related to you, and, only if you give them, year of birth, gender and an alternate contact number.
  • Appointment details: date, time, token number, visit type (new consultation, follow-up or report review) and status; your place in the clinic's waiting queue.
  • Your preferred language and your consent record (the policy version, date and language).
  • Message records: the type, time and delivery status of messages. Not their content.

What we never keep

The text of your messages, voice notes or their transcripts, photos or documents you send, and any health information: symptoms, conditions, medicines or reports. Your messages are read by our software in memory only, to find the booking details (such as a day and time), and are then discarded. Photos and documents are not downloaded. Please discuss health matters with the doctor during your visit.

From clinics and staff

  • Clinic details: name, address, phone number, doctor's name and qualification, consultation timings and settings.
  • Staff names, WhatsApp numbers and roles; one-time login codes (which expire within minutes); a login session cookie; a record of actions taken in the dashboard (for example, "marked a patient arrived").

From Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform)

When a clinic connects its WhatsApp Business Account to ClinicQ through Meta's signup flow, Meta gives us the clinic's WhatsApp Business Account ID, business portfolio ID, phone number ID, display phone number and verified business name, and an access token that lets ClinicQ send and receive messages for that number and manage its message templates. Meta then sends us the clinic's incoming WhatsApp messages and message delivery updates. We use this information only to run ClinicQ for that clinic. We do not receive Facebook passwords, and we do not access the clinic's or anyone's Facebook profile, Pages, friends or advertising data.

3. How we use information

  • To book, confirm, reschedule and cancel appointments, and to send appointment reminders, queue updates ("your turn is near") and follow-up reminders.
  • To run the clinic's waiting queue, dashboard and waiting-room display (which shows a token number and first name only).
  • To let clinic staff log in securely, and to keep the service secure and working (for example, preventing duplicate or forged messages).
  • To understand booking requests written or spoken in English, Hindi or Hinglish. AI models are used only to pick out booking details (such as a day, time or name); they never write replies, and your messages are not used to train any model.

We do not sell, rent or license information; we do not use it for advertising or marketing; and ClinicQ sends no promotional messages, only appointment and queue messages that you or the clinic set in motion.

Legal basis: for patients, your consent, which ClinicQ asks for in WhatsApp before saving anything; you can withdraw it at any time (see section 9). For clinics and staff, our agreement with the clinic.

4. Who we share information with

  • The clinic you message, and its authorised staff, who see their own clinic's patients and appointments only.
  • Service providers that process information for us under contract and only to provide ClinicQ:
    • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) to deliver WhatsApp messages. Meta handles messages under its own terms and may process them outside India.
    • Amazon Web Services, Mumbai (India) region: hosting and database, and Amazon Bedrock to understand booking requests and to convert voice notes to text. These requests are not stored by the provider and are not used to train its models.
  • Authorities, only when required by law.

We do not share information with anyone else, and never with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Where information is stored

ClinicQ stores information on servers in India (Amazon Web Services, Mumbai). WhatsApp messages are carried by Meta, which may process them outside India.

6. How long we keep information

  • Message records (type, time, status; no content): 90 days.
  • Appointment and queue records: names and contact links are removed after 180 days, leaving anonymous visit counts.
  • Patient profiles and numbers: deleted after 12 months without any activity, or straight away when you ask (section 9).
  • Dashboard action records: 365 days. Login codes and sessions: until they expire.
  • Information from Meta about a clinic's WhatsApp account (IDs and access token): deleted within 30 days after the clinic disconnects WhatsApp from ClinicQ or stops using ClinicQ, or sooner on request.

7. How we protect information

All connections use HTTPS. Phone numbers and names are encrypted in our database (AES-256-GCM), and access tokens from Meta are stored encrypted. Staff log in with one-time codes sent on WhatsApp, and each person sees only what their role needs. Messages from Meta are checked for Meta's signature before they are accepted. We would report a personal data breach to the affected clinic, and as required by law, without delay.

8. Children

ClinicQ is not meant to be used by children on their own. A parent or guardian books for a child, using their own WhatsApp number, and gives consent on the child's behalf.

9. Your choices: see, correct or delete your information

Patients

  • Type "my data" in the clinic's WhatsApp chat to see what is kept about you.
  • Type "delete my data" and confirm: your profiles, number and upcoming appointments are deleted straight away. This also withdraws your consent.
  • Type "stop" to stop reminders and updates (and "start" to turn them back on).
  • To correct a name, tell the clinic. To give a different contact number, type "change my number".
  • You can also email {{grievanceEmail}} to ask for any of the above.

Clinics and staff

  • A clinic can disconnect ClinicQ at any time from its Meta business settings (Business settings → Integrations → Connected apps), or by removing ClinicQ as a partner of its WhatsApp Business Account. We then delete the information we received from Meta as described in section 6.
  • To delete a clinic's ClinicQ account and data, or a staff member's details, email {{grievanceEmail}} from the clinic's registered email or WhatsApp number. We confirm by email when it is done.

Full step-by-step instructions: data deletion instructions.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page with a new version number and date. If a change affects how we use your information, we will ask for your consent again before using it in the new way.
