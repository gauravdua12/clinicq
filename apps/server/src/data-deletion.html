How to delete your information from Q-Incolo

Q-Incolo is operated by Incolo Systems. See also the Q-Incolo Privacy Policy.

If you are a patient

  1. Open the WhatsApp chat with the clinic's Q-Incolo number.
  2. Type delete my data.
  3. Tap Yes, delete to confirm.

Your WhatsApp number, the profiles of the people you booked for and your consent record are deleted straight away, and your upcoming appointments are cancelled. You will get a confirmation message. Visit records the clinic keeps for its own counts are made anonymous.

No longer have the chat, or prefer email? Write to {{grievanceEmail}} with the WhatsApp number you used and the clinic's name. We will delete your information and reply to confirm.

If you are a clinic or clinic staff member

  1. Disconnect WhatsApp: in Meta Business Suite, open Business settings → Integrations → Connected apps (or your WhatsApp Business Account's partners) and remove Q-Incolo. This stops Q-Incolo from sending or receiving messages for your number.
  2. Request deletion: email {{grievanceEmail}} from your clinic's registered email or WhatsApp number, saying which clinic (and, for a staff member, which person) to delete.

We delete the information we received from Meta about your WhatsApp account (account and phone number IDs and the access token) within 30 days of disconnection, or sooner on request, together with your clinic's Q-Incolo data if you ask us to. We confirm by email when it is done.
